# inRage

The inRage agency website: it presents the services, the portfolio and the blog, and receives contact requests from prospects.

## Language

**Contact request**:
A message a visitor submits through a site form to reach inRage, carrying their name, e-mail, phone and text. It comes in two variants: a plain contact request and an audit request.
_Avoid_: Lead, message, submission, form entry

**Audit request**:
A contact request asking for the free audit of a website, identified by the site to audit; its message is optional.
_Avoid_: Audit lead, audit form

**Outcome**:
What became of a contact request: invalid, spam, delivered or undeliverable. A request is delivered as soon as one channel, e-mail or notification, accepted it.
_Avoid_: Result, status, response
